smooth-scroll · grain · page-transition

The quiet details.

  • smooth-scrollScrolling glides to a stop instead of jumping. Off on phones, where native scroll feels better.
  • grainA faint moving film grain over everything, so flat colour looks filmed.
  • page-transitionA curtain sweeps over the page when you change pages. Try "Page two" at the top.
  • reduced motionIf your device asks for less motion, or you press "Effects on", every effect switches off and the page stays still and readable.
